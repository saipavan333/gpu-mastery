Interactive Labs

Knowledge you only read decays. The way to make the fundamentals yours is to poke at them until the mental models are muscle memory. These labs come in four flavors: calculators & visualizers for the core mental models, hands-on labs that run real GPU code in your browser, track visualizers that make a specialization's core idea live instead of asserted, and a full study loop — spaced repetition, interview prep, and reference — that keeps it all in your head.

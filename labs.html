Interactive Labs

Knowledge you only read decays. The way to make the fundamentals yours is to poke at them until the mental models are muscle memory. These labs come in three flavors: calculators & visualizers you reason with, hands-on labs that run real GPU code in your browser, and (coming) a spaced-repetition engine that keeps it all in your head.

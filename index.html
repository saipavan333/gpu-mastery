How this course works

Every lesson has two layers: a card with the 80% you need — concept, a worked example with real numbers, key points, two quizzes, and hands-on exercises — then a "Go deeper" half with internals, field gotchas, a production scenario, and seven practice problems ending in the interview closer. Every number is executed, cited to an earlier lesson, or labeled representative.

Progress saves in your browser — mark a lesson complete and the tick persists. The whole site is static and works offline, no dependencies.
